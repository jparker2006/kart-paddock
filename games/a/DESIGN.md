# Parcel Panic design
Friends gather at their desks in daylight for a lively short race; the interface is bright and the world resembles a painted miniature seaside delivery depot.
Full palette: coral dispatch labels, teal water, yellow routing arrows, white interface surfaces, near-black ink. The seed hue is 36 degrees. CSS uses OKLCH. System sans typography, heavy compact title lettering, squared tickets and pill actions. Lobby controls occupy the left while the live 3D island fills the right. Racing clears the central view and anchors information to the edges.
All geometry, texture canvases, kart models and synthesized audio are authored locally. No externally hosted assets.
