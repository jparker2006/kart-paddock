# Product
## Register
product
## Platform
web
## Users
Friends on separate desktop computers, playing a quick keyboard-controlled race together.
## Product Purpose
A complete original online kart race for 2–8 human players, from room creation through three laps and a rematch.
## Positioning
Tiny delivery karts, enormous urgency: an island depot becomes a playful racing circuit.
## Brand Personality
Brisk, sunny, mischievous. Original choices made under the user's autonomous build brief.
## Design Principles
The road is always readable. Friends are recognizable. Connection and race state are truthful. Getting back into the fun is easy.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable contrast, keyboard-operable menus, labeled controls, text plus color for items, optional sound, reduced UI motion. Gameplay requires real-time vision and keyboard control.
